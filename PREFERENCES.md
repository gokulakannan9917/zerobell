# Preferences

- Purpose: live hackathon pitch demo on a projector, for judges and restaurant owners.
- Look: clean, modern dark mode (slate / charcoal background), high contrast.
- Status colours: green = Available / Serving, amber-red = urgent 15-second greeting alert, blue = cleaning needed.
- Layout: restaurant floor simulator and two realistic smartwatch faces side by side.
- Watch faces: clear, high-contrast "Attending" button and a ticking 00:15 countdown.
- Must have: "Guest Left" after a table is served, which sends a blue "Clean Table" alert to the watches (Sit → Attend → Leave → Clean → Available).
- Must have: top bar with live metrics: Average Response Time and Tables Turned.
- Screen language: English.
